# honestly, concerning.

*Date: 03 Jun, 2026*  
*Author: Aditya Diundi*  
*Source: [diundi.bearblog.dev](https://diundi.bearblog.dev/1925/)*

---

time isn’t fast or slow.  
it’s neither mine nor yours.  
it isn’t even its own.  
time doesn’t know about time.  
time doesn’t know how cruel it can be.  

time was unaware  
when i forgot,  
neither was it friendly  
when i was prompt.  

my lifetime is determined  
and is not enough.  
so do i run?  

do i run only to be left behind  
by what we call time?  

oh, how i wish to be like time,  
unburdened by a mind.  

but unlike time,  
i sort of own myself.  

honestly, concerning.  

— Aditya
